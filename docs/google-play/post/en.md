Glow is now on Google Play ☀️

A pocket fan that praises every win. Write down what you did, from washing the dishes to landing the job, and one of five voices cheers you on: a warm friend, a granny, a sports commentator, a wise owl or a butler.

• A jar that fills with painted glass marbles, one per win, and rolls them when you tilt the phone
• An «I feel bad» room for hard moments, with a breathing circle
• A translator from self-criticism into kind words
• Kind words to keep, an evening of pride, 16 orders and a weekly report

Free, no ads, no account, no internet. Everything stays on your phone.

Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.glow
GitHub: https://github.com/bigdevwhale/glow
