Most of what we do every day goes unnoticed, especially by ourselves. The inner critic speaks up the moment something goes wrong and stays quiet when things go right. Glow is the other voice. Tell it about a win, any win, and it cheers.

✨ PRAISE FOR EVERY WIN
• Write what you did and how big it felt: tiny, solid or huge
• The praise, the confetti and the vibration grow with the win
• Quick ideas help on days when "got out of bed" is the win
• Home, body, work, mind, people, courage, self-care, hobby or something else

🫙 A JAR OF WINS
• Each win drops in as a painted glass marble, coloured by its kind
• Tilt or shake the phone and the marbles roll
• Tap one to remember the win and what was said about it
• A new jar every month, and the colours are yours to choose

🎙 FIVE VOICES
A warm friend, a granny, a sports commentator, a wise owl mentor or a butler. Each one praises, comforts and says goodnight in its own way, and each has a hand-painted portrait.

☀️ PRAISE OF THE DAY
A new one every morning, in a notification and a home-screen widget. Keep it, ask for another, share it.

☂️ I FEEL BAD
A dark, quiet room with drifting sparkles, the kindest words one by one, a soft heartbeat and a circle to breathe with.

🔁 SELF-CRITICISM TRANSLATOR
Write the harsh thought. Glow names the thinking trap (all-or-nothing, name-calling, comparing, shoulds and more) and offers a kinder way to say it. Fully offline.

💌 KIND WORDS
Keep compliments from real people, with a screenshot if you like, and read them in shuffle mode on hard days.

🌙 EVENING OF PRIDE
Three things you're proud of today, collected in a pride diary.

🏅 ORDERS AND A WEEKLY REPORT
• 16 orders with three degrees each: bronze, silver and gold medals
• A certificate of honour to share
• A streak that never burns out: miss a day and the jar simply waits
• Every Sunday, a report on how great you were this week

ENGLISH AND RUSSIAN
Every screen and every line of praise is written natively in both languages. In Russian, Glow asks how to speak to you, so it says «ты сделал» or «ты сделала», never «сделал(а)».

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no analytics, and no internet permission at all. Your wins stay on your phone; Settings → Backup saves everything to one file you keep. Free and open source.

Glow is a kind companion, not therapy. If things have felt heavy for a long time, please talk to someone you trust or a professional.
