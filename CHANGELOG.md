## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-16

The first release.

### Praise
- "I've got a win!": write down anything you did, say how big it felt (tiny, solid or huge), and get praised for it. The confetti, the glow and the vibration grow with the win.
- A praise of the day every morning, on the home screen, in a notification and in a home-screen widget. Keep the ones you like, ask for another, share them.
- Five voices to choose from: a warm friend, a granny, a sports commentator, a wise mentor and a butler. Each has its own words for every size of win, for hard moments and for the evening.
- Quick ideas for wins in seven areas: home, body, work, mind, people, courage and self-care.

### The jar
- Every win drops into a glass jar as a coloured stone, one jar per month. Tilt or shake the phone and the stones roll; tap one to remember the win and what was said about it.
- A list of the month's wins by day, filtered by area. Edit or delete any win, or move it to another day.

### Hard moments
- "I feel bad": a dark, quiet room with drifting sparkles, the voice's kindest words one by one, a soft heartbeat and a breathing circle.
- The self-criticism translator: write the harsh thing your inner critic says, and Glow names the thinking trap (all-or-nothing, name-calling, comparing, shoulds, catastrophising and more) and offers a kinder way to say it. Works fully offline.
- Kind words: keep the nice things people say, with an optional screenshot, and read them one at a time in shuffle mode.

### Evening and progress
- The evening of pride: three things you're proud of today, collected in a pride diary.
- Days in a row with wins, a streak that never burns out.
- A weekly report on how great you are, with a chart, your strongest area, your best day and the huge wins, shareable as a picture.
- 16 orders with three degrees each, from the Order of Small Things to the Order of Quiet Courage, with a certificate of honour to share.

### Everything else
- English and Russian. In Russian, Glow asks whether to speak to you in the masculine or the feminine, so there is no "(а)" anywhere.
- Morning, evening and Sunday reminders, light and dark themes, a JSON backup with screenshots included.
- No account, no ads, no internet access.
